Admission Essay – Sofia Martínez Rivera

Since my early years, I have been fascinated by how numbers reveal the hidden patterns that shape our world. During high school, I discovered that economics and data science are not only disciplines of logic and precision but also tools for understanding human behavior and global systems. This realization inspired my ambition to pursue the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science at the University.

I am drawn to the University’s commitment to innovation, integrity and global impact. Its interdisciplinary curriculum and emphasis on analytical thinking perfectly match my goals. I aspire to become an economist who uses data not just to predict trends but to design equitable and sustainable policies. The combination of economics and data analysis offers the opportunity to translate theory into evidence-based solutions.

Throughout my academic journey, I have sought to merge creativity and analysis—whether by founding my school’s Data Science Club or participating in MUN debates that required both logic and empathy. I believe that education should empower us to make decisions that improve communities, not just markets.

Joining the University means joining a community that values leadership through knowledge and responsibility. I am eager to contribute to that environment, bringing my curiosity, discipline and drive to make a measurable difference.

Sincerely,
Sofia Martínez Rivera
