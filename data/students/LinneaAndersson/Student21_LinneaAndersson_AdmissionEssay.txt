Admission Essay – Linnea Andersson

I have always been fascinated by the relationship between numbers and human behavior. Economics allows us to understand choices, while data science provides the tools to measure them. This combination is why I wish to study the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science at the University.

The program’s interdisciplinary approach aligns perfectly with my interests in sustainable growth and quantitative research. I want to use data-driven insights to design policies that balance economic efficiency with environmental responsibility. The University’s global perspective and emphasis on innovation reflect my academic and personal goals.

As President of my school’s Economics Society, I have led projects exploring how data can inform decisions about renewable energy. My internship at SEB Bank taught me how analytics shape financial forecasting. These experiences have strengthened my analytical mindset and my desire to connect theory with practical outcomes.

At the University, I hope to expand my knowledge through collaboration with peers from diverse backgrounds. I want to contribute curiosity, integrity and a deep commitment to responsible economic progress.

Sincerely,
Linnea Andersson
