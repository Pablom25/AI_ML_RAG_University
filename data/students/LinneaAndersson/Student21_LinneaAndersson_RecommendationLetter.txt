Letter of Recommendation – Linnea Andersson

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my pleasure to recommend Linnea Andersson for admission to the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science. I have had the privilege of teaching Linnea in Economics and supervising her work in the Economics Society at Kungsholm Gymnasium.

Linnea is one of the most analytical and disciplined students I have encountered. Her ability to apply mathematical reasoning to economic and environmental questions is exceptional. During her research on Sweden’s green transition, she demonstrated both intellectual curiosity and technical precision.

Her leadership as President of the Economics Society has been exemplary. She encourages collaboration while maintaining high academic standards. Linnea’s volunteer work with Save the Baltic shows her commitment to combining data analysis with real-world impact.

I am confident that Linnea will excel in your program and make valuable contributions to your academic community through her analytical rigor and sense of purpose.

Sincerely,
Mr. Johan Lindström
Head of Economics Department
Kungsholm Gymnasium, Stockholm
johan.lindstrom@kungsholm.edu.se
