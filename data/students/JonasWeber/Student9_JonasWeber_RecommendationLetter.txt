Letter of Recommendation – Jonas Weber

To the Admissions Committee,

I am pleased to recommend Jonas Weber for admission to the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science. As his Mathematics and Economics teacher at Ludwigsgymnasium Munich, I have had the privilege of witnessing his exceptional intellectual ability and analytical precision.

Jonas is highly skilled in applying quantitative reasoning to real-world problems. His data-driven approach to understanding economic behavior demonstrates both creativity and technical expertise. During his internship at Siemens, he contributed to an optimization model that was later implemented for testing—a remarkable achievement for a high school student.

He is also a thoughtful and reliable team player. As President of our Economics Club, Jonas has inspired his peers to explore current global issues with curiosity and rigor. His leadership style is collaborative, confident and grounded in respect.

I have no doubt that Jonas will excel in your challenging program and bring an analytical yet empathetic perspective to your academic community.

Sincerely,
Dr. Martina Schubert
