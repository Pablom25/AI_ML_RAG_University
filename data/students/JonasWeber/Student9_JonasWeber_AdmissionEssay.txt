Admission Essay – Jonas Weber

Economics is the science of choices, and data is its language. I became fascinated by how information can reveal economic patterns and guide better decisions when I built my first predictive model for energy consumption in my neighborhood. That project inspired me to pursue the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science at the University.

The program’s combination of theory and practical analysis perfectly fits my academic interests. I want to understand not only how economies grow but how they can grow sustainably and inclusively. The University’s emphasis on ethics and innovation reflects my belief that data must be used responsibly.

As President of my school’s Economics Club, I have learned how to analyze global markets and communicate complex ideas clearly. My internship at Siemens showed me how technology can optimize resources and improve decision-making. These experiences strengthened my determination to pursue a career in economic research and policy analysis.

At the University, I look forward to collaborating with peers who are equally curious about how data can solve social and economic challenges. I am eager to contribute my analytical mindset, curiosity and commitment to evidence-based progress.

Sincerely,
Jonas Weber
