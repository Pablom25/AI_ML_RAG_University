Admission Essay – Deniz Kaya

Ever since I helped organize a local entrepreneurship fair at age fifteen, I have been fascinated by how ideas turn into business realities. I realized that successful ventures require more than creativity—they need structure, leadership and purpose. This understanding inspired me to apply for the Bachelor in Business Administration at the University.

The program’s focus on innovation and sustainability aligns with my goals. I want to learn how to build businesses that create value both economically and socially. The University’s emphasis on ethical leadership and global collaboration reflects the kind of education I seek.

As Co-Founder of the Young Entrepreneurs Society, I led a team that designed and marketed eco-friendly accessories made from recycled materials. This experience taught me how to manage a team, balance creativity with financial discipline and overcome challenges. My internship at PwC strengthened my analytical and organizational skills, giving me insight into corporate operations.

At the University, I hope to collaborate with international peers and develop as a business leader who values innovation and integrity. I am eager to contribute enthusiasm, creativity and determination to a community that shapes future entrepreneurs.

Sincerely,
Deniz Kaya
