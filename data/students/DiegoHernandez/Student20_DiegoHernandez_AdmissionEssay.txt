Admission Essay – Diego Hernández

From an early age, I have been inspired by how entrepreneurship can transform ideas into solutions that improve lives. Growing up in Monterrey, surrounded by a thriving business community, I learned the importance of leadership, collaboration and innovation. These experiences motivated me to pursue the Bachelor in Business Administration at the University.

The program’s emphasis on global business strategy and ethical leadership aligns perfectly with my career aspirations. I aim to create sustainable business models that promote social responsibility. I believe that business should serve both economic and human development, and I want to learn how to achieve that balance.

As Founder of the Youth Entrepreneurs Network, I have led initiatives connecting young innovators with mentors and local companies. My internship at FEMSA taught me how theory and practice converge in real corporate settings. Both experiences reinforced my desire to study in an environment that values entrepreneurship and integrity.

At the University, I hope to grow as a leader who understands the global economy and uses business as a force for positive change. I am eager to contribute my creativity, cultural perspective and determination to a diverse academic community.

Sincerely,
Diego Hernández
