Letter of Recommendation – Diego Hernández

To the Admissions Committee,

I am honored to recommend Diego Hernández for admission to the Bachelor in Business Administration. I have taught Diego in Business Studies and Economics at Instituto Tecnológico de Monterrey and supervised his work as Founder of the Youth Entrepreneurs Network.

Diego stands out for his initiative, creativity and leadership. He combines analytical precision with a deep sense of responsibility. His entrepreneurial project, which connects students with local startups, demonstrates his ability to turn ideas into tangible results. His teamwork, adaptability and perseverance make him a natural leader.

I am confident that Diego will thrive in your rigorous and international academic environment. He has the vision, energy and values that align perfectly with your program.

Sincerely,
