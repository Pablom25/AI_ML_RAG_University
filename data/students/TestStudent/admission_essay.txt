Growing up in Madrid, I became fascinated by how public decisions shape the everyday life of citizens. 
When my neighborhood introduced a participatory budgeting initiative, I volunteered to help organize 
community discussions. This experience showed me how data, economics, and public engagement intersect.

My goal is to study Economics and Data Analysis to contribute to better-designed policies. 
I am especially passionate about housing affordability, social mobility, and how cities can respond 
to rapid population changes. During my time as a Research Assistant, I saw firsthand how strong data 
can empower decision-makers to implement fairer solutions.

I believe your university's focus on interdisciplinary learning, civic engagement, and real-world projects 
aligns closely with my values. I want to be part of a community that not only studies economic problems, 
but actively works to solve them.

What defines me most is my commitment to helping others. Whether mentoring younger students or organizing 
local initiatives, I am motivated by seeing positive change in the people around me.
