Letter of Recommendation – Aisha Mwangi

To the Admissions Committee,

It is with great enthusiasm that I recommend Aisha Mwangi for admission to the Bachelor in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence. I have had the pleasure of teaching Aisha in Computer Science and supervising her independent research projects at Brookhouse International School.

Aisha demonstrates an exceptional capacity for critical thinking and innovation. She approaches problems with both precision and creativity, often designing practical solutions to real-world issues. Her machine learning project for local weather prediction was one of the most advanced works I have seen at the secondary level.

She is also a natural leader, having founded a coding mentorship initiative for younger students. Her initiative reflects both technical mastery and a strong sense of social responsibility. Aisha’s balance of intellect, integrity and humility makes her an ideal candidate for your program.

I am confident that Aisha will not only thrive in your rigorous environment but also contribute meaningfully to your academic community.

Sincerely,
Mr. David Kamau
Head of Computer Science Department
Brookhouse International School
