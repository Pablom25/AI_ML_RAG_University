Admission Essay – Aisha Mwangi

Technology has always fascinated me because it mirrors the way we think and create. From building my first robot at age thirteen to developing small AI models that predict local rainfall, I have seen how innovation can transform communities. These experiences have shaped my goal to study Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University.

I believe AI should serve humanity by solving real problems. My country, Kenya, faces challenges in agriculture, energy and education that can benefit from intelligent systems. I want to learn how to design algorithms that improve lives without creating inequality or bias. The University’s focus on responsibility, innovation and global awareness aligns perfectly with my values.

My academic background and leadership experience have taught me resilience and teamwork. Through my Robotics Club and volunteer work, I learned that collaboration is key to progress. The program’s multidisciplinary approach will allow me to merge creativity, logic and ethics into one coherent vision.

Joining the University represents an opportunity to learn from global peers and world-class faculty. I am eager to explore how artificial intelligence can be both a science and a force for good.

Sincerely,
Aisha Mwangi
