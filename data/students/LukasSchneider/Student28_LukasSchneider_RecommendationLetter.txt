Letter of Recommendation – Lukas Schneider

To the Admissions Committee,

I am pleased to recommend Lukas Schneider for admission to the Bachelor in Business Administration. As Lukas’s Economics teacher and advisor at Ludwigsgymnasium Munich, I have had the privilege of witnessing his intellectual curiosity, leadership and determination.

Lukas possesses a remarkable ability to combine analytical thinking with creativity. His research on sustainable entrepreneurship demonstrated both academic rigor and innovative thinking. He approaches challenges methodically yet remains open to new ideas, reflecting a true entrepreneurial mindset.

Beyond academics, Lukas has shown exceptional leadership. As Founder of the Young Innovators Club, he has organized events that promote collaboration and real-world business experience among students. His internship at BMW Group further proved his ability to adapt and thrive in a professional environment.

Lukas embodies professionalism, integrity and motivation. I am confident he will excel in your program and contribute meaningfully to your university community.

Sincerely,
Ms. Claudia Becker
Head of Economics Department
Ludwigsgymnasium Munich
