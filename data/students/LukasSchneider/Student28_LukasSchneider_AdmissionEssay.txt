Admission Essay – Lukas Schneider

My interest in business began when I joined a student entrepreneurship fair at age fifteen. The challenge of turning ideas into real solutions fascinated me and taught me the value of innovation, teamwork and responsibility. This experience motivated me to pursue the Bachelor in Business Administration at the University.

I am drawn to the University’s program because it combines academic depth with practical application. I want to learn how business decisions can promote both economic success and social responsibility. The University’s focus on leadership, creativity and ethics aligns with my personal and professional goals.

As Founder of my school’s Young Innovators Club, I have learned how to lead projects, manage resources and motivate others. My internship at BMW Group gave me hands-on experience in marketing and strategy, showing me how collaboration and data analysis shape modern business. These experiences have strengthened my ambition to lead with purpose.

At the University, I look forward to expanding my global perspective and learning from students who share a passion for entrepreneurship. I aim to contribute curiosity, creativity and dedication to a community focused on innovation and impact.

Sincerely,
Lukas Schneider
