Admission Essay – Alessia Romano

Business has always fascinated me as a dynamic field that connects people, ideas and cultures. Watching my parents manage a small family business taught me that entrepreneurship requires not only creativity but also resilience and responsibility. This early experience motivated me to pursue a Bachelor in Business Administration at the University.

The University’s program reflects my academic interests and personal goals. Its focus on innovation, leadership and sustainability perfectly matches my aspiration to build a career in international marketing and business strategy. I am eager to study in an environment that values collaboration and global perspectives.

As President of my school’s Marketing Club, I learned to combine analysis with creativity to design impactful campaigns. My internship at UniCredit allowed me to see how strategy and communication drive decision-making in large organizations. Both experiences helped me develop discipline, adaptability and a sense of purpose.

At the University, I hope to strengthen my understanding of global business challenges while contributing to a diverse community. I want to develop as a leader who uses business as a force for positive change.

Sincerely,
Alessia Romano
