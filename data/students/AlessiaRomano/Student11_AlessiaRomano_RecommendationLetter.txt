Letter of Recommendation – Alessia Romano

To the Admissions Committee,

I am delighted to recommend Alessia Romano for admission to the Bachelor in Business Administration. I have taught Alessia in Business Studies and Economics at Liceo Scientifico Leonardo da Vinci, where she has consistently demonstrated exceptional academic ability and leadership.

Alessia combines analytical precision with creativity and strong communication skills. Her contribution to our Marketing Club has been outstanding, especially in leading a student-run social media campaign that received recognition at a regional competition. She possesses an entrepreneurial spirit supported by discipline and teamwork.

Her commitment extends beyond academics. She volunteers regularly at Milano Food Bank and tutors younger students with patience and empathy. These qualities reflect integrity and social awareness—values that align perfectly with your institution’s mission.

I have complete confidence that Alessia will excel in your program and enrich your university community with her curiosity, dedication and enthusiasm for learning.

Sincerely,
Prof. Marco Conti
Head of Business Studies Department
