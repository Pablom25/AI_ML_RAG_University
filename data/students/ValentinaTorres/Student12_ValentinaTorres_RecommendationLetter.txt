Letter of Recommendation – Valentina Torres

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my great pleasure to recommend Valentina Torres for admission to the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations. I have had the privilege of teaching Valentina in Global Politics and supervising her participation in Model United Nations at St. Catherine’s School.

Valentina is an outstanding student whose analytical skills and commitment to justice set her apart. Her ability to articulate arguments and approach global issues with empathy is exceptional. During MUN conferences, she consistently demonstrated leadership, diplomacy and a genuine interest in understanding diverse perspectives.

She is also a role model outside the classroom. Her volunteer work with Fundación Manos Abiertas reflects her compassion and sense of social responsibility. Valentina combines intellectual rigor with humility and curiosity, making her an ideal candidate for your international and interdisciplinary program.

I am confident that Valentina will contribute significantly to your university community through her integrity, academic excellence and commitment to global change.

Sincerely,
Ms. Mariana López
Head of Social Sciences Department
St. Catherine’s School, Buenos Aires
