Admission Essay – Valentina Torres

I have always believed that law and diplomacy are powerful tools for change. Growing up in Argentina, a country shaped by history and social transformation, I learned that justice is not only written in laws but also built through dialogue and empathy. This belief inspired me to apply for the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations at the University.

The program’s interdisciplinary approach matches my aspiration to understand both the legal frameworks and political processes that define global relations. I want to use this knowledge to advocate for human rights and equitable policies. The University’s focus on ethics, leadership and cultural diversity reflects my personal values.

My experience as a Model United Nations Delegate taught me to think critically and listen actively. Representing Argentina in debates on refugee policy showed me the complexity of international cooperation. My internship at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs deepened my understanding of diplomacy’s challenges and rewards.

At the University, I aim to engage in meaningful discussions, challenge my ideas and grow as a global citizen. I hope to contribute my curiosity, determination and respect for others to a community dedicated to knowledge and justice.

Sincerely,
Valentina Torres
