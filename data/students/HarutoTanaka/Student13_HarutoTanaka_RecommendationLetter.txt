Letter of Recommendation – Haruto Tanaka

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my privilege to recommend Haruto Tanaka for admission to the Bachelor in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence. I have taught Haruto for the past two years in Advanced Mathematics and Computer Science at Tokyo International High School.

Haruto is an exceptional student whose curiosity and precision are remarkable. He consistently demonstrates advanced problem-solving abilities and a natural aptitude for programming. His performance during our robotics competition, where his team reached the national finals, reflects both leadership and creativity.

What distinguishes Haruto is his humility and teamwork. As Captain of the Robotics Club, he ensures that every member contributes and learns. He also volunteers with Code the Future, teaching programming to children, which highlights his generosity and commitment to community impact.

Haruto’s discipline, integrity and innovation make him an ideal candidate for your program. I am confident that he will thrive academically and contribute significantly to your university community.

Sincerely,
Ms. Aiko Nakamura
Head of Computer Science Department
