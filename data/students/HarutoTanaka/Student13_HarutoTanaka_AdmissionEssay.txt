Admission Essay – Haruto Tanaka

My passion for technology began when I built a simple robotic arm using recycled materials at age fourteen. That small project showed me how code could transform ideas into action. Since then, I have been determined to study Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence to explore how machines can enhance human potential.

The University’s program stands out for its interdisciplinary approach and ethical focus. I am especially interested in machine learning and its applications in healthcare and environmental monitoring. I believe technology should serve society, and I want to be part of the generation that develops AI responsibly.

As Captain of my school’s Robotics Club, I have learned the value of teamwork, resilience and continuous learning. My internship at Sony R&D exposed me to professional AI research and inspired me to pursue innovation on a global scale. These experiences reinforced my commitment to using technology for meaningful impact.

At the University, I hope to collaborate with students from diverse backgrounds to create solutions that connect technology, humanity and creativity. I am excited to contribute curiosity, dedication and an international perspective to your academic community.

Sincerely,
Haruto Tanaka
