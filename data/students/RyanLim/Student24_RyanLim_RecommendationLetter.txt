Letter of Recommendation – Ryan Lim

To the Admissions Committee,

I am honored to recommend Ryan Lim for admission to the Bachelor in Business Administration. As Ryan’s Business Management teacher and advisor at Anglo-Chinese School (Independent), I have witnessed his exceptional dedication, creativity and leadership.

Ryan consistently demonstrates strategic thinking and a strong understanding of global business concepts. His analytical work on sustainability in Southeast Asian markets was among the best I have reviewed. He approaches challenges with both intellect and curiosity, combining logic with innovative solutions.

Beyond his academic excellence, Ryan shows remarkable leadership. As Co-Founder of the Student Investment Club, he created initiatives that encouraged peers to learn about finance and ethical investing. His work at Grab Holdings during his internship showed professionalism, adaptability and initiative.

I am confident that Ryan will excel in your program and contribute significantly to your university’s community. His drive, creativity and integrity make him a perfect fit for your institution’s values.

Sincerely,
Ms. Angela Tan
Head of Business Studies Department
Anglo-Chinese School (Independent), Singapore
