Admission Essay – Ryan Lim

Entrepreneurship has always fascinated me because it transforms ideas into impact. Growing up in Singapore, I have seen how innovation can drive growth while addressing social and environmental challenges. This perspective inspired me to apply for the Bachelor in Business Administration at the University.

The University’s focus on leadership, creativity and global awareness aligns with my academic goals. I am eager to explore how businesses can operate responsibly while maintaining competitiveness. I want to learn how to create sustainable models that contribute positively to society.

As Co-Founder of my school’s Student Investment Club, I gained hands-on experience in analyzing market trends and making ethical investment decisions. My internship at Grab Holdings taught me how data and collaboration shape strategic planning. These experiences strengthened my ambition to lead with integrity and innovation.

At the University, I hope to grow as a business leader who understands both financial logic and social responsibility. I look forward to contributing my energy, global perspective and entrepreneurial spirit to your academic community.

Sincerely,
Ryan Lim
