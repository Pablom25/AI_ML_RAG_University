Letter of Recommendation – Olivia Carter

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my honor to recommend Olivia Carter for admission to the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations. I have taught Olivia in Advanced Political Science and supervised her leadership as Secretary-General of our Model United Nations program at Boston Latin School.

Olivia is one of the most intellectually gifted and dedicated students I have encountered. Her analytical depth, combined with eloquence and empathy, makes her an exceptional candidate for your program. She consistently demonstrates the ability to examine global issues from multiple perspectives and propose reasoned, ethical solutions.

Beyond academics, Olivia’s leadership is outstanding. She organized a regional MUN conference hosting over 300 students, managing logistics and diplomatic discussions with professionalism. Her internship at the U.S. Department of State reflects her maturity and ambition to serve in international relations.

Olivia’s intelligence, humility and commitment to justice embody the values your university promotes. I am confident she will make an excellent addition to your academic community.

Sincerely,
Dr. Mark Henderson
Head of Political Science Department
Boston Latin School
mark.henderson@bls.edu
