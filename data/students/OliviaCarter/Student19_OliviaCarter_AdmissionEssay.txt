Admission Essay – Olivia Carter

My passion for international relations began when I attended my first Model United Nations conference. Debating issues of global inequality and climate change showed me that diplomacy requires both intellect and empathy. Since then, I have aspired to study Law and International Relations to understand how institutions can protect human rights and foster cooperation.

The University’s Dual Degree program offers the ideal foundation for this goal. Its interdisciplinary curriculum will allow me to develop legal reasoning while gaining insight into global politics. I am particularly interested in international human rights law and its role in ensuring accountability in an increasingly complex world.

As Secretary-General of my school’s MUN, I learned to manage conflicts, encourage collaboration and communicate under pressure. My internship at the U.S. Department of State exposed me to the realities of policy-making and strengthened my desire to pursue diplomacy. These experiences taught me that leadership requires patience, integrity and cultural awareness.

At the University, I hope to grow as a scholar and as a citizen of the world. I want to contribute to discussions on justice and equality while learning from peers who share a commitment to positive global change.

Sincerely,
Olivia Carter
