Admission Essay – Aleksander Nowak

From my first experience building a robot at age fourteen, I became fascinated by how code can transform ideas into reality. That experience inspired me to explore computer science and artificial intelligence as tools for creativity and problem-solving. I am applying to the Bachelor in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence to deepen my technical understanding and expand my global perspective.

The University’s program offers the perfect combination of innovation, research and ethical responsibility. I am particularly drawn to its focus on applied learning and AI for sustainability. I want to develop technologies that make cities smarter and more environmentally efficient.

Leading my school’s Robotics Club taught me that success depends not only on technical skill but on teamwork and persistence. My internship at Google Poland showed me how collaboration drives innovation in real-world settings. These experiences have strengthened my motivation to pursue this field.

At the University, I hope to learn from peers who share my passion for technology and its potential to shape the future. I aim to contribute curiosity, discipline and creativity to an environment that values progress and collaboration.

Sincerely,
Aleksander Nowak
