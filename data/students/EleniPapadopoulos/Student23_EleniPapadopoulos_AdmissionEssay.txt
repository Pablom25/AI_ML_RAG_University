Admission Essay – Eleni Papadopoulos

My decision to study Law and International Relations stems from a belief that justice and diplomacy are the foundations of peace. Growing up in Greece, a crossroads of cultures and ideas, I became deeply aware of how history and policy shape human lives. I want to dedicate my career to promoting fairness and understanding between nations.

The University’s Dual Degree program perfectly combines the disciplines I value most. Its global perspective and interdisciplinary structure align with my goal of contributing to international organizations that defend human rights and strengthen cooperation. I am particularly interested in European law and migration policy, areas that reflect both my cultural heritage and academic passion.

Through my experience as Debate Team Captain, I learned to balance conviction with empathy. My internship at the Greek Ministry of Justice deepened my understanding of legal processes and the importance of ethical governance. Volunteering with the Hellenic Red Cross taught me that humanitarian work begins with listening and respect.

At the University, I hope to engage with diverse perspectives and challenge myself to think critically and compassionately. I aim to contribute to a community that values dialogue, responsibility and the pursuit of justice.

Sincerely,
Eleni Papadopoulos
