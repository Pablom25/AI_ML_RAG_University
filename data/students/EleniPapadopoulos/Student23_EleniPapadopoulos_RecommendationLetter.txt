Letter of Recommendation – Eleni Papadopoulos

To the Admissions Committee,

I am pleased to recommend Eleni Papadopoulos for admission to the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations. I have had the privilege of teaching Eleni in Political Science and serving as her advisor during her internship at the Greek Ministry of Justice.

Eleni is a remarkable student who combines academic excellence with empathy and leadership. Her analytical skills are outstanding, and her ability to express complex ideas clearly makes her a natural communicator. She approaches each topic with curiosity and respect for diverse perspectives.

As Debate Team Captain, Eleni has demonstrated exceptional leadership, guiding her peers through research and strategy while fostering an atmosphere of collaboration. Her volunteer work with the Hellenic Red Cross shows her commitment to humanitarian values and civic responsibility.

Eleni exemplifies intellectual rigor, compassion and perseverance. I am confident that she will excel in your program and contribute positively to your international academic community.

Sincerely,
Dr. Andreas Nikolaidis
Head of Political Science Department
