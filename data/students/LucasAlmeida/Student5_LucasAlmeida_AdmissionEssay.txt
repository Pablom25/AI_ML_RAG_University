Admission Essay – Lucas Almeida

When I first learned to use Python to analyze public data, I realized that numbers could tell human stories. I discovered that data is not only technical but deeply social, revealing patterns behind unemployment, inequality and environmental impact. This perspective inspired me to pursue the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science at the University.

I am drawn to this program because it bridges the analytical rigor of economics with the innovation of data science. The University’s emphasis on research, global awareness and responsibility reflects the kind of academic environment I seek. I want to learn how to transform complex information into solutions that improve people’s lives.

My experience leading the Data Analytics Club and interning at Itaú Bank strengthened my ability to interpret data ethically and collaboratively. I learned that data-driven insights are only powerful when they are communicated clearly and used responsibly.

At the University, I hope to expand my global perspective and contribute to projects that promote social progress. I aspire to become an economist who uses data not just to describe the world but to make it fairer and more sustainable.

Sincerely,
Lucas Almeida
