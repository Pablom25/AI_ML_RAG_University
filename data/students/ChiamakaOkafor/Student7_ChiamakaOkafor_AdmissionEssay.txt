Admission Essay – Chiamaka Okafor

When I was twelve, I witnessed a family friend wrongly accused in a local dispute. Watching how justice depended on access and education made me realize the importance of fair legal systems. Since then, I have been determined to study law and international relations to contribute to building institutions that protect rights and promote equality.

The Dual Degree in Law and International Relations is the perfect program for me because it unites two fields I deeply care about. It will allow me to understand how global governance and legal frameworks interact to shape societies. The University’s emphasis on ethics, leadership and international cooperation reflects my personal goals.

Through my experience as Debate Team President and as a delegate in the Model African Union, I have learned how dialogue and understanding can solve conflicts. My internship at Human Rights Watch Africa strengthened my belief that advocacy requires not only passion but evidence-based reasoning.

At the University, I want to grow as a leader who bridges cultures and promotes fairness through diplomacy and law. I am eager to learn from diverse peers and to contribute to the academic community with my voice, empathy and commitment to justice.

Sincerely,
Chiamaka Okafor
