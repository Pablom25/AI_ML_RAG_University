Letter of Recommendation – Chiamaka Okafor

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my honor to recommend Chiamaka Okafor for admission to the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations. I have had the privilege of teaching her in Government and Civic Education and supervising her involvement in our Debate Team at Corona Secondary School.

Chiamaka is a thoughtful, articulate and highly motivated student. Her capacity to connect legal principles with real-world issues is exceptional for her age. During our Model African Union sessions, she demonstrated both diplomacy and leadership, always ensuring discussions remained respectful and solution-oriented.

Her compassion for social justice and equality stands out. She volunteered extensively with the Justice for Youth Foundation, where she educated children about their rights in underrepresented communities. Her empathy, coupled with her intellectual discipline, makes her an ideal candidate for your interdisciplinary program.

I have no doubt that Chiamaka will thrive in your academic environment and contribute meaningfully to your university’s mission of global engagement and ethical leadership.

Sincerely,
Mr. Tunde Balogun
Head of Social Sciences Department
Corona Secondary School, Lagos
tunde.balogun@coronasecondary.edu.ng
