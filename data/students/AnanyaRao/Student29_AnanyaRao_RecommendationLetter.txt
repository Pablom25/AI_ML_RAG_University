Letter of Recommendation – Ananya Rao

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my pleasure to recommend Ananya Rao for admission to the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science. I have had the privilege of teaching Ananya in Advanced Economics and supervising her as President of the Data Analytics Club at Indus International School.

Ananya is an exceptional student whose intellectual ability and curiosity set her apart. Her work demonstrates analytical rigor and a deep understanding of how data informs economic policy. Her independent research on inflation and income inequality in India displayed creativity, technical accuracy and strong communication skills.

Beyond academics, Ananya shows impressive leadership. As President of the Data Analytics Club, she designed initiatives that encouraged students to use technology for problem-solving. Her empathy and organizational skills make her a respected leader in our school community.

Ananya’s analytical mindset, discipline and compassion make her an ideal candidate for your program. I have no doubt she will excel academically and contribute meaningfully to your university community.

Sincerely,
Dr. Neha Menon
Head of Economics Department
