Admission Essay – Ananya Rao

My interest in economics and data science began when I observed how statistics can reveal patterns that shape entire economies. I became fascinated by how data can turn complex social problems into actionable insights. This realization inspired me to apply for the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science at the University.

The program’s interdisciplinary approach aligns perfectly with my academic interests. I want to explore how machine learning and econometrics can contribute to public policy and economic sustainability. The University’s focus on innovation, ethics and global awareness reflects my goals of applying data for social good.

As President of the Data Analytics Club, I learned how collaboration and curiosity drive problem-solving. My internship at Deloitte India gave me practical experience in analyzing markets and understanding business strategies. These experiences have reinforced my passion for data-driven decision-making.

At the University, I hope to deepen my analytical skills and contribute to research that addresses real-world challenges. I am eager to learn in a diverse community that values creativity, precision and purpose.

Sincerely,
Ananya Rao
