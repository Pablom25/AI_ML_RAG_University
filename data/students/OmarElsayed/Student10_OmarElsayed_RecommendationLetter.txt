Letter of Recommendation – Omar El-Sayed

To the Admissions Committee,

It is a great pleasure to recommend Omar El-Sayed for admission to the Bachelor in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence. I have had the honor of teaching Omar in Advanced Computer Science and supervising his robotics team at Cairo American College.

Omar is a brilliant student who combines technical precision with creativity. His leadership as Robotics Team Captain has inspired his peers to think innovatively and work collaboratively. He consistently pushes beyond the curriculum, building complex algorithms and exploring the ethical implications of AI.

His character is as impressive as his intellect. Omar demonstrates humility, responsibility and a sincere desire to apply technology for social good. His initiative in developing educational tools for children with learning difficulties shows compassion and innovation working together.

I have no doubt that Omar will excel in your program and enrich your academic community with both skill and integrity.

Sincerely,
Mr. Hassan Farouk
Head of Computer Science Department
Cairo American College
