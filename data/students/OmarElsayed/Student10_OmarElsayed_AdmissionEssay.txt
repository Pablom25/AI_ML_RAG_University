Admission Essay – Omar El-Sayed

My fascination with technology began when I disassembled my first computer at age ten. What started as curiosity turned into a lifelong passion for understanding how machines think and learn. I want to study Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University to transform that curiosity into innovation that benefits people.

The program’s focus on both technology and ethics aligns with my belief that innovation must be responsible. I am particularly interested in developing accessible AI tools for education and healthcare. I see artificial intelligence not as a replacement for human intelligence but as its extension.

As Captain of my school’s Robotics Team, I learned that teamwork and resilience are essential to success. Our team’s projects taught me to manage complexity, communicate clearly and learn from failure. My internship at Vodafone introduced me to the corporate side of innovation, where creativity meets structure.

At the University, I look forward to collaborating with students who share a global vision for technology. I am eager to contribute to an environment that values creativity, integrity and the power of learning through exploration.

Sincerely,
Omar El-Sayed
