Admission Essay – Arjun Patel

The business world has always fascinated me for its power to create innovation and social change. Growing up in Mumbai, I have seen how entrepreneurship can transform lives by generating opportunities. My goal is to study Business Administration to learn how to build companies that combine profitability with purpose.

The University’s Bachelor in Business Administration program aligns perfectly with my vision. Its focus on innovation, global learning and ethical leadership reflects the kind of business education I value. I am eager to explore courses in strategy, management and entrepreneurship that encourage creativity and responsibility.

My experiences founding the Student Entrepreneurship Club and interning at Deloitte taught me that leadership is about teamwork and vision. I learned to communicate ideas clearly, adapt to change and approach business problems with empathy. These lessons continue to shape my goals as a future entrepreneur.

At the University, I hope to join a diverse community that challenges conventional thinking and promotes sustainable impact. I want to gain the knowledge and network to one day lead an organization that creates value not only for shareholders but also for society.

Sincerely,
Arjun Patel
