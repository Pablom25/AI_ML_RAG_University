Admission Essay – Thandiwe Mbeki

I grew up fascinated by how economies shape people’s lives. Watching my community in Johannesburg face challenges of inequality and unemployment inspired me to understand how data and economics can drive change. This motivation led me to apply for the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science at the University.

I am drawn to this program because it combines the analytical depth of economics with the precision of data science. I want to learn how to use data to inform policies that promote equitable and sustainable growth. The University’s focus on innovation, ethics and global awareness reflects my own vision for impactful education.

As President of my school’s Economics Club, I learned how to lead discussions on complex issues while encouraging collaboration. My internship at Standard Bank gave me hands-on experience in analyzing financial data and understanding how insights can support inclusion. These experiences have shaped my goal of working at the intersection of policy and technology.

At the University, I hope to join a diverse community that values creativity and problem-solving. I aim to contribute my analytical mindset, curiosity and commitment to fairness to both academic and social initiatives.

Sincerely,
Thandiwe Mbeki
