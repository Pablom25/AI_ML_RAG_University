Letter of Recommendation – Thandiwe Mbeki

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my privilege to recommend Thandiwe Mbeki for admission to the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science. I have taught Thandiwe in Advanced Economics and supervised her Economics Club initiatives at St. Mary’s School in Johannesburg.

Thandiwe is an exceptional student whose curiosity, discipline and analytical skills set her apart. Her research on microfinance and income inequality displayed remarkable critical thinking and precision. She approaches every problem with an evidence-based mindset, demonstrating both creativity and rigor.

Her leadership extends beyond academics. As President of our Economics Club, she encourages open discussion and collaboration. Her volunteer work with the Ubuntu Youth Foundation reflects her commitment to social progress and inclusion. Thandiwe embodies the values of empathy, integrity and responsibility.

I am confident that she will excel in your rigorous program and make meaningful contributions to your university’s global community.

Sincerely,
Dr. Peter van der Merwe
Head of Economics Department
St. Mary’s School, Johannesburg
peter.vandermerwe@stmarys.edu.za
