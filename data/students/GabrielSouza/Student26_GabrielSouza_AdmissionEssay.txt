Admission Essay – Gabriel Souza

When I first built a simple robot from recycled parts, I realized that technology could be both creative and transformative. Since then, I have been fascinated by how artificial intelligence can improve lives. I want to study Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University to develop the technical and ethical foundations to create meaningful innovations.

The University’s program stands out for its combination of theory, practice and social responsibility. I am especially drawn to the focus on AI ethics and innovation management. I hope to apply what I learn to develop technologies that are efficient, inclusive and sustainable.

As Founder of my school’s Robotics and AI Club, I learned how teamwork transforms ideas into solutions. My internship at IBM Brazil taught me how innovation happens through collaboration and data analysis. These experiences deepened my desire to study in an international environment that challenges me to think critically and act responsibly.

At the University, I hope to explore the intersection of technology and ethics while contributing my creativity, cultural perspective and curiosity to a global learning community.

Sincerely,
Gabriel Souza
