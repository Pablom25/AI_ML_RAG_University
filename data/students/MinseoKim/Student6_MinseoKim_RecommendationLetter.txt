Letter of Recommendation – Minseo Kim

To the Admissions Committee,

It is a privilege to recommend Minseo Kim for admission to the Bachelor in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence. I have taught Minseo in Advanced Computer Science and have supervised his robotics research projects at Seoul Foreign School.

Minseo is one of the most exceptional students I have encountered. His ability to combine technical knowledge with creative problem-solving is outstanding. During our AI research module, he developed a model that improved image recognition accuracy by 12 percent, demonstrating both innovation and discipline.

He also leads by example. As Robotics Club Leader, Minseo fosters collaboration and inclusion among team members. His initiative to teach younger students coding reflects maturity and empathy. Minseo’s passion for learning and his respect for others make him an excellent representative of your institution’s values.

I strongly recommend him as a candidate who will excel academically and contribute positively to your university community.

Sincerely,
