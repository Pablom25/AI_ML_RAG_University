Admission Essay – Minseo Kim

My fascination with artificial intelligence began when I built a simple chatbot in ninth grade. Since then, I have been captivated by how machines can learn and adapt, reflecting aspects of human thought. I want to study Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University to deepen my understanding of how technology can serve people and society.

The University’s program stands out for its focus on innovation and ethics. I am particularly interested in exploring how AI can improve accessibility for individuals with disabilities. Combining engineering with empathy is what motivates me most. The program’s interdisciplinary structure will allow me to merge coding, design and human understanding.

As leader of my school’s Robotics Club, I learned that progress comes through teamwork and persistence. Working with others to build functional prototypes taught me how to transform ideas into tangible outcomes. My internship at Seoul National University further confirmed my desire to pursue research that has social impact.

At the University, I hope to collaborate with peers from around the world who share a passion for technology and purpose. I am excited to contribute creativity, discipline and curiosity to an environment that values innovation and responsibility.

Sincerely,
Minseo Kim
