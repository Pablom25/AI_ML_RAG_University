Letter of Recommendation – Javier Morales

To the Admissions Committee,

I am delighted to recommend Javier Morales for admission to the Bachelor in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence. I have had the privilege of teaching Javier in Computer Science and Mathematics at Institut Montserrat in Barcelona.

Javier is one of the most innovative and disciplined students I have worked with. His ability to apply theoretical knowledge to practical challenges is exceptional. During our robotics competition, he led a team that designed an autonomous robot capable of real-time decision-making. His initiative and technical skill are matched by creativity and persistence.

He also shows a deep commitment to collaboration and social contribution. His volunteer work with Code4Youth demonstrates his belief that technology should empower others. Javier’s integrity, curiosity and humility make him a perfect fit for your program’s rigorous and forward-thinking environment.

I strongly recommend Javier without reservation. He will be an asset to your university, both as a student and as a future innovator.

Sincerely,
Dr. Elena Ruiz
Head of Computer Science Department
Institut Montserrat, Barcelona
