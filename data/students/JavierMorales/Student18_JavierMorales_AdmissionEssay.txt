Admission Essay – Javier Morales

When I wrote my first line of code at age thirteen, I was amazed by the idea that logic could create something new. That moment sparked a passion for computer science that has only grown stronger. I want to study Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence at the University to explore how technology can improve human lives.

The program’s emphasis on innovation and ethical responsibility aligns with my personal vision. I am especially interested in artificial intelligence applications in education and accessibility. I believe technology should be inclusive and transparent, serving society rather than replacing it.

Through my experience as Co-Founder of my school’s Coding Club, I learned the importance of teamwork and creative problem-solving. My internship at IBM Spain gave me insight into how AI can transform industries while raising new ethical questions. I want to learn how to address those challenges responsibly.

At the University, I hope to collaborate with students who share my curiosity for technology and its human impact. I aim to contribute energy, creativity and commitment to an environment that values progress through innovation and empathy.

Sincerely,
Javier Morales
