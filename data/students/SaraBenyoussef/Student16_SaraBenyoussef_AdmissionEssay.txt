Admission Essay – Sara Benyoussef

Growing up in Morocco, I have always been fascinated by how dialogue and diplomacy can bridge cultures and resolve conflicts. My participation in Model United Nations opened my eyes to the power of negotiation and global cooperation. These experiences inspired me to apply for the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations at the University.

I am drawn to this program because it unites my two passions—law and international affairs. I believe that sustainable peace and justice require both legal understanding and diplomatic engagement. The University’s commitment to ethics, global leadership and critical thinking reflects the education I seek.

Through my internship at the Ministry of Foreign Affairs, I learned the importance of detail, analysis and teamwork in policy-making. As MUN Chair, I developed communication and leadership skills while learning to listen to diverse viewpoints. My volunteer work with refugee children reminded me that real diplomacy begins with empathy and respect.

At the University, I hope to contribute to discussions on international law and human rights while learning from peers who share a global vision. I want to develop as a future diplomat dedicated to fairness, dialogue and sustainable development.

Sincerely,
Sara Benyoussef
