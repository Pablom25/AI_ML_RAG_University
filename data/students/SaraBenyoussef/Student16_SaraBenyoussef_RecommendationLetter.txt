Letter of Recommendation – Sara Benyoussef

To the Admissions Committee,

I am pleased to recommend Sara Benyoussef for admission to the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations. I have taught Sara in Political Science and supervised her Model United Nations leadership at Lycée Lyautey in Casablanca.

Sara is an exceptional student who demonstrates intellectual maturity and cultural sensitivity. Her analytical skills and ability to mediate complex discussions make her stand out among her peers. She consistently approaches international issues with a balance of empathy and logic, a rare and valuable combination.

Her leadership as MUN Chair reflects strong organizational and communication skills. She also shows genuine dedication to social causes through her volunteer work with Association Amal, helping refugee children adapt to new environments. Sara embodies responsibility, curiosity and respect for diversity.

I am confident that Sara will excel in your program and contribute meaningfully to your global academic community.

Sincerely,
Mr. Karim El Idrissi
Head of Social Sciences Department
Lycée Lyautey, Casablanca
