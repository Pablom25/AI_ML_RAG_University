Letter of Recommendation – Mariam Hassan

To the Admissions Committee,

I am delighted to recommend Mariam Hassan for admission to the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science. I have had the privilege of teaching Mariam in Economics and supervising her Girls in Data Club at The British International School in Cairo.

Mariam is a highly motivated and analytical student with exceptional problem-solving skills. Her research on inflation patterns in North Africa demonstrated impressive use of data modeling and critical analysis. She combines academic excellence with leadership and initiative.

Her contributions extend beyond academics. As Founder of the Girls in Data Club, she has inspired many students to explore STEM subjects, promoting confidence and inclusion. Her volunteer work shows empathy and a strong sense of community service. Mariam is thoughtful, disciplined and intellectually curious.

I am confident that Mariam will excel in your program and make valuable contributions to your university’s academic and social life.

Sincerely,
Mr. Ahmed Khalil
Head of Economics Department
