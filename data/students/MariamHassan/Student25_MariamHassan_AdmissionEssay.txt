Admission Essay – Mariam Hassan

My passion for economics and data science began when I noticed how numbers influence every decision we make, from personal budgets to national policies. I realized that combining economic understanding with data analysis could drive meaningful change. This is why I want to pursue the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science at the University.

The program’s focus on analytical thinking and ethical application of technology aligns with my academic goals. I aspire to use data-driven insights to design economic solutions for sustainable growth in developing countries. The University’s emphasis on innovation and global perspective matches my desire to study in a diverse, forward-looking environment.

As Founder of the Girls in Data Club, I have learned the importance of leadership and mentorship in education. My internship at Banque Misr taught me how data supports financial strategy and development. These experiences strengthened my belief that knowledge and technology can empower communities.

At the University, I hope to grow as a data-driven economist who values precision, equity and progress. I aim to contribute my curiosity, dedication and cultural perspective to a global learning community.

Sincerely,
Mariam Hassan
