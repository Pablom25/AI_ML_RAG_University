Letter of Recommendation – Ethan Miller

To the Admissions Committee,

I am pleased to recommend Ethan Miller for admission to the Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science. I have taught Ethan in Advanced Mathematics and supervised his Economics research projects at Sydney Grammar School.

Ethan is a student of remarkable intellect and discipline. His ability to interpret data and apply it to real-world problems is impressive. His analytical reasoning and attention to detail make him stand out.

I am confident that Ethan will excel in your program and contribute meaningfully to your academic community through his analytical rigor and innovative mindset.

Sincerely,
