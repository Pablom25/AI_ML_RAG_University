Admission Essay – Ethan Miller

My passion for economics began when I tried to understand how the COVID-19 pandemic affected global supply chains. What started as curiosity soon turned into a deep interest in using data to explain economic behavior. I want to study Economics and Data Science at the University to explore how analytical thinking can improve decision-making in business and policy.

The University’s Dual Degree program offers the perfect balance between economic theory and computational practice. I am especially interested in learning how to use data visualization and predictive modeling to make complex systems understandable. I believe that economics, supported by data, can help create fairer and more efficient societies.

As Founder of the Data Science Club, I have learned to communicate technical concepts clearly and to lead collaborative projects. My internship at Commonwealth Bank taught me how data informs financial strategy and risk management. Both experiences reinforced my ambition to combine quantitative reasoning with real-world problem-solving.

At the University, I hope to expand my skills, challenge my assumptions and work alongside students who share my enthusiasm for data-driven progress. I want to contribute my analytical mindset and curiosity to a global academic environment.

Sincerely,
Ethan Miller
