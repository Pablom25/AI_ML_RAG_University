Admission Essay – Julien Lefèvre

My interest in law and international relations began when I volunteered with Amnesty International at the age of fifteen. Reading testimonies of people affected by conflict and injustice taught me that international law is not only about rules but about protecting human dignity. Since then, I have aspired to become a lawyer working in international diplomacy.

The Dual Degree in Law and International Relations offers exactly the intellectual and ethical framework I seek. The program’s interdisciplinary approach will allow me to understand both the legal mechanisms and the political dynamics that shape global affairs. I am particularly drawn to the University’s focus on integrity, innovation and global collaboration.

My experience as a Model United Nations Chair and my internship at the French Ministry of Foreign Affairs strengthened my ability to negotiate and analyze complex issues. These experiences showed me that diplomacy requires not only knowledge but empathy and resilience. I aim to use these skills to promote justice and peaceful cooperation.

Studying at the University represents a unique opportunity to learn among diverse peers who share the ambition to make a meaningful global impact. I am eager to contribute my perspective, curiosity and commitment to ethical leadership.

Sincerely,
Julien Lefèvre
