Letter of Recommendation – Julien Lefèvre

To the Admissions Committee,

I am delighted to recommend Julien Lefèvre for admission to the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations. I have had the pleasure of teaching Julien in Political Science and supervising his participation in our school’s Model United Nations program at Lycée Saint-Louis.

Julien is among the most intellectually mature and dedicated students I have taught. His ability to connect political theory with current international events is remarkable. He consistently demonstrates analytical rigor and diplomatic tact in every discussion, balancing strong convictions with openness to diverse perspectives.

Beyond academics, Julien shows genuine leadership and empathy. As Vice President of the Student Council, he coordinated civic projects promoting inclusion and global awareness. His initiative and integrity make him an ideal candidate for an interdisciplinary and international program like yours.

I am confident that Julien will contribute actively to your university community and uphold its values of excellence, global vision and ethical responsibility.

Sincerely,
Ms. Claire Dubois
Head of Political Science Department
Lycée Saint-Louis, Paris
