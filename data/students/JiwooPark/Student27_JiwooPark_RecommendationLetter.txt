Letter of Recommendation – Jiwoo Park

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my pleasure to recommend Jiwoo Park for admission to the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations. I have had the privilege of teaching Jiwoo in Global Politics and supervising her as President of the Debate Club at Seoul Foreign School.

Jiwoo is an outstanding student whose intellectual depth and empathy make her a natural leader. She demonstrates exceptional analytical skills and a strong commitment to justice. Her ability to connect complex legal and political ideas is remarkable for a high school student.

As Debate Club President, Jiwoo has shown initiative, discipline and collaboration. Her leadership is defined by fairness and integrity. She also balances her academic rigor with social awareness, volunteering regularly with Amnesty Youth Korea to promote equality and civic responsibility.

I am confident that Jiwoo will excel in your program and contribute meaningfully to your university community through her intellect, compassion and global perspective.

Sincerely,
