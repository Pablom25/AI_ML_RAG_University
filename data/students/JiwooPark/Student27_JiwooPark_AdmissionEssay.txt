Admission Essay – Jiwoo Park

From an early age, I have been fascinated by how law and diplomacy shape the world we live in. Growing up in South Korea, I witnessed how international cooperation influences progress and stability. These experiences inspired me to pursue the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations at the University.

The program’s interdisciplinary nature reflects my academic and personal aspirations. I want to study how law can serve as a foundation for fairness and how diplomacy can bridge cultural differences. The University’s emphasis on ethics, global leadership and policy-making aligns with my desire to contribute to international development and peace.

As President of my school’s Debate Club and a participant in Model United Nations, I have learned that leadership requires both empathy and resilience. My internship at the National Assembly taught me the importance of analytical thinking in shaping policy. These experiences have deepened my understanding of governance and civic responsibility.

At the University, I hope to collaborate with peers from diverse backgrounds and engage in projects that address global issues. I want to grow as a future leader committed to justice, dialogue and cooperation across borders.

Sincerely,
Jiwoo Park
