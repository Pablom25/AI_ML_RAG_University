Letter of Recommendation – Amira Bensalem

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my great pleasure to recommend Amira Bensalem for admission to the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations. I have had the privilege of teaching Amira in History and Political Science and advising her in the Model United Nations program at Lycée International Honoré de Balzac.

Amira is an outstanding student whose intellectual curiosity and moral integrity set her apart. She approaches every topic with analytical depth and genuine empathy. Her ability to connect historical events with contemporary political challenges is exceptional for her age.

Her leadership extends beyond the classroom. As Vice President of the Debate Club, she has inspired thoughtful dialogue on global issues. Her volunteer work with Amnesty International demonstrates her strong commitment to human rights and civic responsibility.

Amira’s maturity, resilience and compassion make her an ideal candidate for your program. I am confident she will thrive in your rigorous academic environment and contribute meaningfully to your international community.

Sincerely,
Dr. Philippe Laurent
Head of Social Sciences Department
Lycée International Honoré de Balzac, Paris
