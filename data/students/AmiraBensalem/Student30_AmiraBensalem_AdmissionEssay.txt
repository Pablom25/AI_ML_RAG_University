Admission Essay – Amira Bensalem

I have always been fascinated by how dialogue and law can shape societies. Growing up between France and Algeria, I developed an appreciation for cultural diversity and the importance of mutual understanding. These experiences inspired me to pursue the Dual Degree in Law and International Relations at the University.

The program’s global approach perfectly matches my goals of combining legal knowledge with diplomacy. I want to study how international institutions can protect human rights and promote equality. The University’s emphasis on ethics, leadership and global collaboration aligns with my vision of law as a tool for social progress.

Through my work as Vice President of the Debate Club, I learned that persuasion and empathy are equally important in discussions about justice. My internship at the French Ministry of Justice deepened my understanding of how policy is translated into action. These experiences have strengthened my determination to study law in an international context.

At the University, I hope to develop as a leader who uses knowledge to foster cooperation and respect between cultures. I am eager to contribute curiosity, dedication and a multicultural perspective to a diverse academic community.

Sincerely,
Amira Bensalem
