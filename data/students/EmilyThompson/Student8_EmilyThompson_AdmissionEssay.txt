Admission Essay – Emily Thompson

From a young age, I was fascinated by how businesses shape communities and drive innovation. Watching my parents build a small family enterprise taught me that success requires not only strategy but empathy and resilience. This experience inspired my desire to study Business Administration at the University.

I am drawn to the University’s focus on entrepreneurship, innovation and ethical leadership. Its curriculum aligns perfectly with my goal of starting a business that promotes sustainability and social impact. I want to learn how to manage organizations that balance profit with purpose.

As Co-Founder of my school’s Sustainable Startups Club, I have experienced the challenges of developing real projects with limited resources. This role taught me that leadership is about listening, collaboration and adaptability. My internship at RBC reinforced my interest in finance and strategic management, showing me how data-driven decisions can improve long-term success.

At the University, I hope to join a diverse community that values creative problem-solving and global awareness. I am eager to contribute my ideas, discipline and ambition to an environment that fosters innovation with integrity.

Sincerely,
Emily Thompson
