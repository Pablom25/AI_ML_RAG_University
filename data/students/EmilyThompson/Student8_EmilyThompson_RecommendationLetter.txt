Letter of Recommendation – Emily Thompson

To the Admissions Committee,

It is my pleasure to recommend Emily Thompson for admission to the Bachelor in Business Administration. I have had the privilege of teaching Emily in Economics and supervising her as Co-Founder of our Sustainable Startups Club at Bishop Strachan School.

Emily’s leadership and creativity distinguish her among her peers. She has an extraordinary ability to identify business opportunities and transform them into practical solutions. Her recent project on eco-friendly product design demonstrated strategic thinking and a strong sense of social responsibility.

She approaches every task with maturity, precision and enthusiasm. Her teamwork and communication skills were evident during her internship at RBC Bank, where she worked effectively in a professional environment. Emily’s curiosity and integrity make her an excellent fit for your program’s global and ethical focus.

I have no doubt that she will succeed academically and contribute positively to your university’s community of learners and innovators.

Sincerely,
Ms. Sarah Collins
Head of Business Department
Bishop Strachan School, Toronto
