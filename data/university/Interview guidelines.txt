University Admission Interview Guidelines


SECTION 1. INTRODUCTION AND PURPOSE

The purpose of the admission interview is to evaluate the suitability of candidates for undergraduate studies at the University. The interview process aims to assess each applicant’s academic motivation, personal values, communication skills and alignment with the university’s mission and culture of innovation, leadership,and global engagement.


SECTION 2. GUIDELINES FOR APPLICANTS

1. Preparation
- Review your academic background, personal statement and achievements.
- Research the university’s values, programs and teaching philosophy.
- Be prepared to discuss your academic interests, goals and extracurricular activities.

2. Conduct
- Arrive (or connect online) punctually and present yourself in professional attire.
- Address interviewers with courtesy and maintain a respectful demeanor.
- Listen carefully and respond thoughtfully to questions.
- Use clear, structured and confident communication.
- Avoid memorized or overly rehearsed answers.

3. Recommended Content for Responses
- Motivation for applying and choosing your program of study.
- Long-term goals and how the university will help you achieve them.
- Examples of leadership, collaboration or problem-solving experiences.
- Reflection on challenges you have overcome and lessons learned.

4. Common Interview Topics
- Personal background and academic interests.
- Strengths, weaknesses and self-awareness.
- Ethical dilemmas or decision-making experiences.
- Global outlook and adaptability.

5. Post-Interview
- Send a brief thank-you message to the Admissions Office.
- Reflect on your performance and note areas for improvement.
- Await official communication regarding the next steps.


SECTION 3. GUIDELINES FOR INTERVIEWERS

1. Objectives
- Evaluate academic readiness, communication, motivation and cultural fit.
- Create an atmosphere of professionalism and respect.
- Gather insights beyond the written application.

2. Conduct
- Review the applicant’s file before the interview.
- Begin with an introduction and explanation of the interview’s structure.
- Use open-ended questions that invite reflection.
- Avoid bias and ensure equal treatment of all candidates.
- Take concise notes for evaluation purposes.

3. Sample Questions
- What inspired you to apply to this program?
- How do you approach teamwork and collaboration?
- Describe a time you showed initiative or leadership.
- What do you hope to contribute to our university community?


SECTION 4. EVALUATION CRITERIA

Interviewers will assess applicants according to the following criteria:
- Academic potential and intellectual curiosity.
- Communication and interpersonal skills.
- Motivation, maturity and self-awareness.
- Leadership and teamwork capacity.
- Integrity and ethical reasoning.
- Alignment with the university’s mission and values.


SECTION 5. ETHICAL AND PROFESSIONAL STANDARDS
- All interviews must be conducted in a fair, transparent, and confidential manner.
- Interviewers must avoid conflicts of interest or favoritism.
- Applicants must provide truthful and accurate information.
- Any form of discrimination or inappropriate behavior is strictly prohibited.
