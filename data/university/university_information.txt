University Overview

SECTION 1. UNDERGRADUATE DEGREES

The University offers four undergraduate programs inspired by leading international institutions. Each degree is designed to combine academic excellence, professional development and global perspective.

1. Bachelor in Business Administration (BBA)
   - Type: Single Degree
   - Focus: Management, leadership, finance and entrepreneurship.
   - Duration: 4 years

2. Dual Degree in Law and International Relations
   - Type: Dual Degree
   - Focus: Legal reasoning, diplomacy and international policy-making.
   - Duration: 5 years

3. Bachelor in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence
   - Type: Single Degree
   - Focus: Computing, AI systems and data-driven innovation.
   - Duration: 4 years

4. Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science
   - Type: Dual Degree
   - Focus: Economic theory, quantitative analysis and applied data science.
   - Duration: 5 years


SECTION 2. UNIVERSITY VALUES

The University upholds a set of core values that guide its academic and community life:

1. Innovation and Excellence – Encouraging creativity, entrepreneurship and research that shape the future.
2. Global Perspective – Promoting intercultural understanding and international collaboration.
3. Integrity and Responsibility – Ensuring ethical behavior, transparency and social awareness.
4. Collaboration and Leadership – Fostering teamwork, empathy and inclusive leadership.
5. Sustainability and Impact – Committing to positive change for society and the environment.


SECTION 3. ADMISSION REQUIREMENTS BY DEGREE

1. Bachelor in Business Administration (BBA)
   - High school diploma or equivalent.
   - Strong performance in mathematics, economics or social sciences.
   - English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 95+ or IELTS 7.0+).
   - Personal statement outlining leadership and motivation.
   - Admission interview.


2. Dual Degree in Law and International Relations
   - High school diploma with excellence in humanities or social sciences.
   - Strong writing and analytical skills.
   - English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 100+ or IELTS 7.5+).
   - Motivation essay and interview.
   - Model United Nations or debate participation preferred.
   - Admission interview.


3. Bachelor in Computer Science and Artificial Intelligence
   - High school diploma with advanced math and science background.
   - Coding or STEM extracurricular activities recommended.
   - English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 95+ or IELTS 7.0+).
   - Portfolio or technical challenge submission.
   - Admission interview.


4. Dual Degree in Economics and Data Science
   - High school diploma with strong results in mathematics and statistics.
   - Demonstrated interest in economics, finance or analytics.
   - English proficiency (TOEFL iBT 100+ or IELTS 7.5+).
   - Personal essay and interview.
   - Advanced coursework in calculus or programming preferred.
